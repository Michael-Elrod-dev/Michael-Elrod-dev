## FLAGSHIP PROJECTS

### Mimir (currently private)
Session notes for a weekly D&D campaign, named for the Norse god who remembers everything. He sits in Discord voice, records every player on their own track, transcribes the session and writes it up. Then you can ask him anything about the campaign: hundreds of sessions of history and the details specific to each character.
[View Repo](https://github.com/Michael-Elrod-dev/mimir)

### AlmostHadAI
A Twitch chat bot and Windows app for streamers. It talks to your chat, takes Spotify song requests through channel points, and keeps your commands, emotes, quotes and channel stats in one place. You run all of it from the desktop app and any redemption either does what it promised or gives the points back. Mostly built for my own channel, but I'm working on making it a real product with a subscription for other streamers some day. A true all in one chat bot solution.
[View Repo](https://github.com/Michael-Elrod-dev/Twitch-Chat-Bot)
